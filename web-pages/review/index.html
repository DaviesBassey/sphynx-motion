Reviewer Walkthrough

What to Test

Step 1 — Launch & Splash

  • 1 Open the app. A branded dark splash screen appears with the SPHYNX MOTION logo and a loading bar. It transitions automatically to the home feed within 3 seconds.
  • 2 The home feed displays series cards with poster artwork, genre labels, and age rating badges.

Step 2 — Sign In

  • 1 Tap Profile in the bottom navigation. Tap Log In.
  • 2 Enter the demo credentials above. Tap Sign In. A welcome toast and daily reward appear within 1–2 seconds.
  • 3 The profile screen now shows the account name, Soul Token balance (200+), and watch history summary.

Step 3 — Watch a Series

  • 1 Tap any series card on the home feed. The video player opens and begins loading the first episode.
  • 2 Video plays inline (full-screen portrait). Tap to show controls. Swipe up for next episode, swipe down for previous.
  • 3 Tap the Save button in the right rail to add to watchlist. Tap Next to advance to the next episode.
  • 4 Progress is saved automatically and resumes from the same position on next launch.

Step 4 — Soul Tokens & Rewards

  • 1 Tap Profile → Earn Soul. A list of daily reward tasks appears — Daily Login, First Watch, Share Series, Complete E3.
  • 2 Tap any unclaimed task. The Soul Token balance updates in real time. Some tasks will show as already claimed (daily limit).
  • 3 Soul Tokens are a free virtual currency. No purchase is required to earn or use them.

Step 5 — Soul Pass (Subscriptions)

  • 1 Tap the Member tab (centre icon in nav). The Soul Pass subscription screen shows plan benefits.
  • 2 Tap Get Monthly or Get Annual. A screen titled "Unlock Everything" appears listing plan benefits and noting that Soul Pass subscriptions are available via Apple In-App Purchase in version 1.1.
  • 3 No payment is processed in this build. Apple IAP via RevenueCat will be integrated in the v1.1 update. The subscription UI is intentionally non-functional in this initial release.
IAP Note for Reviewer: All subscription and token purchase flows on iOS display an Apple IAP placeholder. No Stripe, PayFast, or external payment gateway is accessible from the iOS app. Web-only payment methods are completely gated behind iOS detection.
Guideline 3.1.1 — In-App Purchase

Payment Compliance

Current Payment Architecture

  • ✓ iOS app: All purchase buttons route to an Apple IAP "coming soon" screen. No external payment method is reachable.
  • ✓ Web prices hidden: ZAR pricing (R89/R59 etc.) is hidden via CSS on iOS. Plan cards show features only, no currency amounts.
  • ✓ No external checkout: Stripe and PayFast are used on the web version only. They are completely unreachable from the native iOS app.
  • ✓ Free content available: All core app features — watching, earning Soul Tokens, Echo Voting, and saving to watchlist — are available without any purchase using the demo account.
