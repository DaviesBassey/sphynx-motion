Help Centre

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?
Open the app, tap Profile in the bottom nav, then tap Create Account. Enter your name, email, and a password. Your account is created instantly.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
On the login screen, tap Forgot password? Enter your email and we'll send a reset link. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes. For further help contact support@sphynxplay.com.
What are Soul Tokens?
Soul Tokens are the in-app currency of SphynxPlay. Earn them by logging in daily, watching episodes, and sharing series. Use them to Echo Vote on episode storylines and unlock premium content. Soul Tokens have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for cash.
How do I earn Soul Tokens?
Go to Profile → Earn Soul. You can earn tokens by: daily login (+10), watching your first episode of the day (+15), sharing a series (+25), and completing milestone episodes (+20). Tokens reset daily for recurring tasks.
What is Echo Voting?
At the end of each episode, Echo Voting lets you choose the direction of the story. Each vote costs Soul Tokens. The community's votes influence future episodes. You can only vote once per episode.
What is Soul Pass?
Soul Pass is the SphynxPlay subscription. Subscribers get access to all series, all episodes, HD streaming, bonus Soul Tokens every month, and early access to new releases. Subscriptions are managed through the App Store or Google Play.
How do I cancel my Soul Pass subscription?
On iPhone: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → SphynxPlay → Cancel. On Android: Google Play → Subscriptions → SphynxPlay → Cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.
Video won't play — what do I do?
Check your internet connection first. If the problem persists: close and reopen the app, check that your iOS/Android is up to date, and try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data. If video still won't play, contact support@sphynxplay.com with your device model and iOS version.
How do I change the app language?
Go to Profile → Language. Select from English, Zulu, Xhosa, Afrikaans, Yoruba, Swahili, French, Arabic, and more. The change applies instantly.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile → Delete Account. You will be asked to confirm twice. Deletion is permanent — all your Soul Tokens, watch history, and profile data will be removed and cannot be recovered. If you need assistance, email support@sphynxplay.com.
How do I report a content issue?
Tap the ⋯ icon on any series card while browsing to report it. Our moderation team reviews all reports within 24 hours. You can also email support@sphynxplay.com.
