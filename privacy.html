§ 01

Overview

SphynxPlay (Pty) Ltd ("SphynxPlay", "we", "us", or "our") operates the SphynxPlay streaming platform, including our mobile application and website at sphynxplay.com (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our Service.

By accessing or using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Service.

Your privacy matters to us. We are committed to handling your personal information responsibly, in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) of South Africa, and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable.

§ 02

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

  • Account registration: name, email address, password, profile photo
  • Profile details: display name, bio, content preferences
  • Communications: messages, support requests, feedback you send us
  • Payment details: processed securely through Apple App Store or Google Play — we never store raw card numbers

Category Examples Purpose
Device Information Device model, OS version, unique device ID App compatibility & security
Usage Data Content watched, watch time, search queries, playback position Recommendations & personalisation

Information from Third Parties

If you sign in using Google, Apple, or another social login provider, we receive your name and email from that provider. We do not receive your password.

§ 03

How We Use Your Data

We use your personal information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide, personalise, and improve the streaming Service
  • Process subscription payments and Soul Token transactions
  • Send account-related notifications and, where you opt in, marketing emails
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations under POPIA, GDPR, and other applicable laws
  • Analyse aggregated, anonymised usage trends to improve content and features

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

§ 04

Sharing Your Information

We only share your information in the following circumstances:

Service Providers

We engage trusted third-party companies to help operate the Service — including cloud hosting, analytics, customer support, and payment processing. These providers access your data only to perform services on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or when necessary to protect our rights, property, or the safety of our users.

Business Transfers

If SphynxPlay undergoes a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

With Your Consent

We may share your information with other parties when you have given us explicit consent to do so.

§ 05

Data Retention

Data Type Retention Period
Account & profile data Until account deletion, then up to 30 days for recovery
Watch history & preferences Duration of account + 6 months post-deletion
Payment records 7 years (statutory requirement)
Log & security data 12 months
Support communications 3 years from last contact

After the applicable retention period, data is securely deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

§ 06

Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Encryption of sensitive data at rest
  • Access controls and role-based permissions for internal systems
  • Regular security reviews and vulnerability assessments

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to keep your credentials confidential.

§ 07

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies on our website and app to:

  • Essential cookies: authentication sessions, security tokens — required for the Service to function
  • Analytics cookies: understand how users interact with our platform (aggregated, non-identifiable)
  • Preference cookies: remember your language, playback, and display settings

You can control non-essential cookies through your device or browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may prevent you from logging in or using core features.

§ 08

Payments & Soul Tokens

Subscription Payments

All subscription billing is handled exclusively through Apple App Store or Google Play Store. SphynxPlay does not store, process, or have access to your payment card details. Refund and billing inquiries are governed by the respective platform's terms of service.

Soul Tokens

Soul Tokens are SphynxPlay's virtual in-platform currency. They are used to unlock premium content and features within the app.

  • Soul Tokens are purchased through Apple In-App Purchase or Google Play Billing
  • Soul Tokens have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred between accounts
  • Unused Soul Tokens may be forfeited upon account termination
  • Purchases are non-refundable except where required by applicable law or the platform's own refund policy

Note: Soul Token purchases are subject to Apple's and Google's refund policies. To request a refund, contact Apple Support or Google Play Support directly.

§ 09

Children's Privacy

The SphynxPlay Service is intended for users aged 16 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us at privacy@sphynxplay.com. We will promptly delete such information upon verification.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account in two ways:

  1. In-App: Go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account and follow the on-screen instructions.
  2. By Email: Send a deletion request to privacy@sphynxplay.com from your registered email address. We will process your request within 30 days.

Account deletion removes your profile, watch history, and preferences. Payment records are retained for the statutory period as noted in Section 5.
