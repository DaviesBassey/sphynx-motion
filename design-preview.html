07   Decisions Log

Date Decision Rationale
2026-05-12 Aesthetic: Obsidian Cinema safe Dark is table stakes for streaming. Content pops on OLED. Matches "Netflix/Disney+ level" north star.
2026-05-12 Display: Fraunces (editorial serif) safe Drama titles need literary gravity. Free on Google Fonts. Achieves Canela feel without license cost.
2026-05-12 Body: Geist Sans safe Neutral, clean, modern. Zero cultural baggage. Reads long descriptions without fatigue.
2026-05-12 Text: #F0EDE8 warm white (not #FFF) safe Pure white on near-black causes eye strain on OLED. Warm off-white is softer and more cinematic.
2026-05-12 Soul Tokens: ◈ 2,840 in JetBrains Mono risk No badge, no icon. A number and a symbol — quiet status, not gamification. Feels like a real economy. New users may need tooltip to understand ◈.
2026-05-12 Hero: single still frame (no carousel) safe One show, full attention. Cinematheque over buffet. Removed carousel risk in favour of typographic tokens as the sole bold departure.
2026-05-12 Token gold: #C9A84C (old coin, not Vegas) safe Warmer, more earned than bright gold. Carries gravity. Doesn't cheapen the dark aesthetic.
